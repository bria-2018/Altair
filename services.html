Commercial Sign & Lighting Services

Altair Sign & Light is a sign company in Atlanta that has provided state-of-the-art signs and lighting in the local area for over 25 years. Our team of industry professionals will create, install, and maintain the perfect sign and lighting for your company.

What We Do

Signs, Lighting & Property Services Under One Roof

We offer a wide variety of professional quality signs and lights, from electronic boards and window graphics to upgraded security lighting and property management services. Our work comes with an outstanding warranty on both labor and materials, and Altair employs a full-time permit expediter in-house to ensure accurate, timely permitting and local zoning compliance.
