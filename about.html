Altair Sign & Light: Sign Design & Manufacturing Company

Altair Sign & Light is a sign company in Atlanta who has provided state of the art sign and lighting in the local area for over 25 years. We offer a wide variety of professional quality signs and lights from electronic boards and window graphics to upgraded security lighting and property management services. Our team of industry professionals will create, install, and maintain the perfect sign and lighting for your company, working hard to ensure your unique business needs are realized.

Our client’s satisfaction is always our top priority. We offer competitive pricing and discounts. Our work comes with an outstanding warranty on both labor and materials. Altair employs a full time permit expediter in-house to ensure accurate, timely permitting and local zoning compliance.

You need a trusted sign design & manufacturing company to handle your commercial signage needs. Whether you’re a national brand or a local business, we are your professional sign and lighting solution!
