Professional Sign Removal for Atlanta Businesses

Taking a sign down looks simpler than putting one up. A lit cabinet sign carries live electrical, a pylon sign has a foundation and a weight you cannot judge from the ground, and a channel letter set is bolted through the facade into structure you cannot see until it is off.

Doing it wrong leaves you with torn stucco, exposed conduit, a damaged parapet, or a liability claim. A sign removal company should turn up with the right lift, disconnect the power properly, and leave the wall ready for whatever comes next.

When You Need Sign Removal

Most sign removals start with one of a few events: a rebrand, a closing or relocation, a lease ending, or a landlord requiring the space returned to base condition. We also get called for storm-damaged and outdated signs, and for signs that have fallen foul of a city code change. Redevelopment work is the other common one, where everything comes off before demolition.

Our Sign Removal Process

  1. Request and site assessment. We look at access, height, mounting, and whether the sign is lit. Photographs usually settle most of it.
  2. Written quote. You get the scope in writing, including disposal and wall repair, before anyone books a date.
  3. Permits and disconnection. Where a permit is needed we handle it, and a licensed electrician disconnects and caps any live circuits.
  4. Safe removal. Bucket truck or crane as the job requires, with the site cordoned off. Our crews follow OSHA aerial lift practices.
  5. Wall repair and cleanup. Holes filled, surface patched, debris cleared, and the sign disposed of, recycled, or returned to you.

Safety, Licensing, and Insurance

Signs come down over sidewalks, parking areas, and customer entrances, so the safety plan matters as much as the removal. We use a licensed electrician on every illuminated job, and pull permits through the City of Atlanta Office of Buildings or the relevant county office where one is required.

Sign Removal Cost in Atlanta

There is no flat rate. Any sign removal company quoting one over the phone has not asked enough questions. The cost of signs removal comes down to these factors:

  • Size and height of the sign
  • Type and weight, and whether it is illuminated
  • Equipment needed, such as a bucket truck or crane
  • How easy the site is to access
  • How much wall repair is required
  • Disposal and permit fees

A single set of unlit letters at ground level and a 30-foot illuminated pylon are different jobs. We quote after a site assessment so the number you get is the number you pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does sign removal cost?

There is no standard price. Cost depends on the size and height of the sign, its weight and type, whether it is illuminated, the lift equipment needed, site access, wall repair, and disposal or permit fees. We quote after a site assessment rather than guessing over the phone.

Can you remove a sign attached to a leased building? Do I need landlord permission?

Yes, we remove signs from leased premises regularly. You will normally need written landlord approval first, and most leases also require the wall returned to its original condition. We can speak to the property manager directly and include the repair in the quote.

Will you repair the wall after removal?

Yes, and it is included in the quote rather than added later. We fill mounting holes, patch stucco, brick, EIFS, or metal panel, and color match the paint. Many sign removal companies only take the sign down and leave the patching to you.

Do you remove illuminated and electrical signs?

Yes. A licensed electrician disconnects and caps the circuit before the sign comes down, and we make the connection point safe behind the wall. Never let an unlicensed crew cut power to a lit sign, as live conduit behind a facade is a serious hazard.

Do I need a permit to remove a sign in Atlanta?

Sometimes. It depends on the jurisdiction, the sign type, and whether structural or electrical work is involved. We check the requirement for your specific address and handle the application where one is needed, so you are not chasing the paperwork yourself.

What happens to the old sign? Can I keep it?

Your choice. We can dispose of it, recycle the metal and electrical components, or return it to you intact if you want to reuse or store it. Tell us before the removal date so the crew brings the right transport.
