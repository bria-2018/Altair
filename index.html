Over 25 Years Building Metro Atlanta Business Skyline

Altair Sign & Light, Inc., is an Atlanta-based sign company specializing in the design, manufacturing, installation, and service of commercial signage for businesses throughout Metro Atlanta. For more than 25 years, our in-house designers, fabricators, and licensed technicians have created signage built to withstand Georgia’s weather while representing your brand clearly and accurately at street level.

Whether you’re outfitting a single storefront or managing a multi-location rollout, you get the same dedicated approach: one team and one point of contact from initial concept through final installation. With a full-time permit expediter on staff, we manage surveys, applications, and zoning compliance for you—keeping the permitting process off your plate.
