Channel Letter Sign Design, Manufacturing & Installation

Channel letters are 3-dimensional elements used to prominently display and illuminate the name of a business. Channel letter signs in Atlanta are not rare to come by, but finding the right company to trust with your business’s channel letter sign is tough to do. With Altair, you can feel assured that your Atlanta channel letter sign will be expertly handled every step of the way. From design to installation, you can trust Altair with all of your channel letter sign needs. Plus, there is no limit to the benefits that come with adding a channel letter sign to your business’s look. Some of the many factors that contribute to this dependability include:

A dependable signage solution, channel letters are:

  • Made from weather-resistant aluminum
  • Painted and fitted with desired lighting components (such as neon or LEDs)
  • Fitted with a translucent plastic face to ensure proper closure
  • Able to be mounted flush with the wall, or stud mounted to stand out from the wall

Our sign and light experts build and install eye-catching channel letters that will draw attention to your business. We offer indoor & outdoor channel letter signs.
